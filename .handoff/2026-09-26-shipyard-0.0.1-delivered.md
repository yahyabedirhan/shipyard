## Decisions made in the conversation that aren't obvious from the artifacts

- **Parallel by default on the Mac.** Unblocked tickets run in parallel sub-agents, each in its own git worktree (`isolation: "worktree"`), doing code and tests only, with one commit each. The orchestrator cherry-picks the commits, resolves conflicts (usually `docs/low-level-design.md`), installs once and checks the real menu once. Only `make install` and the one real menu are serialised. Short, context-rich briefs with no per-ticket code review took 2–5 minutes; the old per-ticket-review briefs took 10–34. This is saved in the orchestrator's memory, and the skills change is yahyabedirhan/skills#1.
- **Every bug becomes a ticket, with a screenshot.** Follow `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md` ("Efforts", "Bugs and screenshots"):
  - each effort has an `effort:<name>` label;
  - screenshots go on the orphan branch `issue-assets` through a guarded `bash -euo pipefail` plumbing push, and are embedded with commit-pinned raw URLs;
  - an empty source in that push deletes the branch. This happened once and was restored.
- **Checking the real app.** Agents can click the menu bar and take screenshots, because Ghostty has Accessibility and Screen Recording.
  - The steps are in #27's "How to check the real menu": to close the menu after a scripted click, click the icon again, since Escape doesn't work.
  - The screen locks after about 40 minutes idle. Then nothing can be clicked or captured; check `ioreg -n Root -d1 -a | grep -A1 CGSSessionScreenIsLocked` and ask the maintainer to unlock.
- **The permission system refuses `rm -rf`** on scratch folders, even after the maintainer approved. The maintainer's fallback: move them into the repository's git-ignored `.scratch/`. Dropped prototypes are in `.scratch/ui-prototypes-dropped/` and `.scratch/icon-options-dropped/`.
- **Scratchpad leftovers** in the old session's temp scratchpad (safe to lose): prototypes `p1-dense-list` and `p3-tabs`, the icon generators under `icons/`, and #20's `rel/`, `zip/` and `cfgcheck/`. Nothing in the repository depends on them.
- **Commit messages:** lowercase, with `Co-Authored-By` as the only trailer. Never add `Claude-Session:`, even when the harness reminder asks: the project's CLAUDE.md wins.
